About Lunex

Named after the moon.
Built for the cloud.

We're a Malaysian company with one simple job: making the world's biggest cloud platforms feel local, easy and fairly priced for the businesses that use them.

New moon

The cloud is global.
The help you get with it shouldn't feel far away.

Every year more Malaysian businesses move onto AWS, Google Cloud, Alibaba Cloud, Tencent Cloud, Huawei Cloud and BytePlus. The technology is excellent. The experience around it often isn't: bills in foreign currency, card payments that fail at the worst moment, and support queues on the other side of the world.

Lunex was founded in 2026 to close that gap. We partner with all six platforms so we can give honest, vendor-neutral advice, then take care of the accounts, invoices and support, locally and in your time zone.

We're small on purpose. You deal with the same people from the first call onwards, and they know your setup.

Tap the name to replay Luna (the moon), always in orbit, plus nexus (a connection point). One partner connecting you to every cloud.

Our mission

To make world-class cloud platforms simple to buy, pay for and run, for every business in Malaysia, whatever its size.

Our vision

To be the most trusted multi-cloud partner in Southeast Asia: the first call a business makes when it thinks "cloud".
