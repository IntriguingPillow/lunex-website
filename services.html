Account setup & transfer

Opening a cloud account sounds simple until verification stalls, the wrong person ends up as the owner, or everyone shares one admin login. We open new accounts under our partner programmes, or move your existing ones under Lunex, and set them up properly from day one.

What you get

  • New accounts on any of the six clouds
  • Separate logins and roles for each team member
  • Security baseline: MFA, alerts and audit logging
  • Customer discounts to help lower your cloud costs

How it runs

  1. Tell us who needs accessCompany details and your team list.
  2. We open or transferWe handle the provider's paperwork and checks.
  3. Hand-overYou get the logins, plus a short guide on who can do what.

02

Billing & top-up in your currency

Global providers usually bill in US dollars on a credit card. That means exchange-rate swings, card limits, and services paused when a payment fails. With Lunex, every cloud you use is combined into one invoice that you can settle in Ringgit or in the currency that works best for your business.

What you get

  • One monthly invoice covering every cloud
  • Settle in MYR, USD or other supported currencies
  • Payment by bank transfer
  • Prepaid top-up option for tighter budgets
  • Monthly spend report, broken down by project
  • Budget alerts before costs run over

How it runs

  1. Link your accountsYour cloud accounts are billed through Lunex.
  2. Use as normalNothing changes for your engineers.
  3. Pay one invoiceIn your preferred currency each month, with a clear usage report.
Ask us about billing & currencies

Architecture advice

Each provider will tell you its own cloud is best. We work with all six, so our only goal is a setup that suits your workload, your users and your budget, even if that means mixing more than one cloud.

What you get

  • Review of your current setup or new idea
  • Side-by-side comparison of suitable clouds and regions
  • Architecture diagram you can share with your team
  • Monthly cost estimate in your preferred currency
  • Recommendations for scaling, backups and uptime

How it runs

  1. Discovery sessionWe learn what you're building and for whom.

  3. Final designA clear plan you can build yourself, or have us help with.

04

Migration

Moving from office servers to the cloud, or from one cloud to another, is like moving house: most of the risk is in the planning. We map everything first, rehearse on a copy, and switch over when it suits your business.

What you get

  • Inventory of servers, databases and dependencies
  • Step-by-step migration and rollback plan
  • Test migration before the real one
  • Data and application transfer
  • Support during and after the switch-over

How it runs

  1. AssessWhat moves, what stays and what gets replaced.
  2. RehearseA full test run on a copy of your system.
  3. Switch overPlanned around your quietest hours.
Ask us about migration
05

Cost & security check-ups

Cloud bills tend to grow quietly, and small settings left open can turn into big problems. We review your accounts regularly and explain what we find in plain language, ranked by what matters most.

What you get

  • Idle and oversized resources flagged
  • Savings options such as reserved capacity and right-sizing
  • Checks for public storage, open ports and weak access
  • Backup and recovery review
  • Short written report with a prioritised to-do list

How it runs

  1. Read-only accessWe look, and change nothing without your approval.
  2. ReviewCost and security checks across your accounts.
  3. Walk-throughA call to go through the findings, then we help fix them if you want.
Book a cost & security check-up
